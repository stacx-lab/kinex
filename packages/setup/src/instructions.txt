# Device setup

Use /onboarding to configure a workspace, /register to prepare an unloaded device,
/calibrate to resolve calibration requests, and /unregister to remove a device.
Load setup-device when adding a device or repairing its driver. Follow the ordered
setup guide roots and the runtime type conventions. Setup tools work with real
hardware and applicable simulator profiles.

Use calib_plan to inspect missing facts and calibration requests. Preserve unknown
facts as ??? and record requests through calib_write. Match calibration skills to
their resolves selectors; clear requests explicitly only after validating the facts.
Registration leaves new devices unloaded. Activation uses /load or the device tool
with its existing permissions. Planning and readiness do not certify safe motion.
