# Onboarding and calibration

Use /onboarding to configure a workspace and /calibrate to resolve calibration
requests. Device registration, removal, provisioning and setup-device belong to
core. Read its Device guide roots for device Setup/Profile and Use conventions.
These workflows support both real hardware and simulators.

Use calib_plan to inspect missing facts and calibration requests. Preserve unknown
facts as ??? and record requests through profile_write. Match calibration skills to
their resolves selectors; clear requests explicitly only after validating the facts.
Registration leaves new devices unloaded. Activation uses /load or the device tool
with its existing permissions. Planning and readiness do not certify safe motion.
