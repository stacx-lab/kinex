Prepare the workspace robot's device profiles and drivers. Scope: $ARGUMENTS

Run calib_plan first. Use existing profiles, local models, SDK metadata and supplied
facts before asking questions. For simulated robots, use the exported workspace;
fill missing facts from the simulator configuration instead of interviewing about
hardware. Keep unresolved facts as ??? and queue their reasons with profile_write.

## Device model

A device is a named control or observation endpoint. Choose its type by its
interface, independently of the robot's morphology:
- joint: absolute positions for an ordered joint set, including arms, lifts or legs;
- velocity: declared velocity axes and optional posture, including wheeled or legged bases;
- gripper: scalar control; dexterous_hand: finger-joint control; suction: binary control;
- rgb, rgbd, imu, force_torque: distinct sensor contracts;
- model: a served inference endpoint; other: a custom device.

One body can expose several control endpoints. The operator selects non-overlapping
devices for use. Whole-body control needs a concrete supported contract; do not
invent a type or infer that joint-controlled legs require a locomotion policy.
Create only endpoints that exist; an arm need not have a gripper.

Each device has devices/<name>/profile.yaml under .kinex/:
- access: connection and driver;
- intrinsics: control/feedback facts, model or sensor calibration, and local frames;
- extrinsics: mounting transform or measured pose reference.
Models have no extrinsics. World has access and a driver for read/wait on its
clock, plus optional intrinsics for spatial references, bounds and surfaces.
World has no extrinsics.

Address the canonical frame by device name and local frames as device:frame.
intrinsics.frames values are fixed xyz+xyzw poses, null for moving transforms
from read.frame_poses, or ??? for uncalibrated fixed transforms. extrinsics.mount
connects devices. Frame trees may have independent roots; world is optional.
A tool's canonical frame may be its TCP, or it may declare a separate local TCP.

## Interview and persist

Ask only for missing facts or choices. Batch related questions using the question
tool and offer "I don't know". Recommend a literal value only when evidence supports
it; do not ask the user to reconfirm information they already supplied.

When a fact is unknown, record ??? and queue {item,note} with profile_write kind=needed.
Continue with other actionable items. Preserve source provenance: user for supplied
facts, introspected for extracted facts, calibration for measured results, unknown
for gaps. Clear needs_calibration with null only after the entire item is resolved.

Save facts after each phase. Re-run calib_plan after writes and follow its applicable
section dependencies. Store embodiment-specific work in the project's .kinex/:
devices/<name>/ for profiles/drivers, lib/ for shared helpers, skill/ for procedures,
and docs/ for durable SDK notes. Leave the Kinex installation unchanged.

## Setup sequence

1. Identity: record id, name, platform, kind and known make/model in workspace.yaml.
   Morphology belongs in identity.kind. Reuse a supplied id or derive a short one.
2. Inventory: identify the actual control modes and sensors, then write one stub per
   endpoint with name, type and loaded:false. Reuse the user's device names.
3. Per-device setup: load setup-device and follow the selected instance/type guide.
   Use its starter profile and implement the driver. Record device-owned URDF paths
   and base_link/tip_link selections when applicable; home is a separate named
   configuration, not inferred from the URDF. Prefer existing prepared profiles
   over another setup interview.
4. Validate profiles, imports and drivers statically. Registration does not read
   hardware or activate services. The device tool load action or /load activates a prepared device;
   verify status/read only after it is loaded. Record unavailable access as a gap.
5. Record known safety caps, keep-out zones and e-stop details in manifest safety
   fields/notes. Add world geometry only when requested or already measured.
   Joint reach estimates do not establish environment bounds or support surfaces.

Finish with what is prepared, unresolved items and their matching skills, and the
calib_plan readiness result. Point to /load for activation and /calibrate for queued
measurements. Unloading preserves profiles and removes devices from task readiness.
Later devices use /register; existing profiles can be updated without repeating
completed setup.
