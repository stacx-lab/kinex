## Compose the embodiment

Describe actual non-overlapping endpoints by interface: URDF joint arms, lifts and
dexterous hands, locomotion bodies (wheeled, legged or humanoid) with declared
modes, URDF grippers, suction tools, cameras and other sensors. An arm need not have a gripper. Quadrupeds need not expose raw
leg joints; never invent a locomotion policy or a whole-body control interface.
Morphology is identity.kind, not an execution contract. It describes the whole robot
and must be arm, mobile, quadruped, humanoid or other. Use other when no category
fits; ??? is not accepted for this field.
