Read the calibration plan in dependency order. Devices contribute applicable access,
intrinsics and extrinsics items. Access checks connection facts, driver presence and
declared actions; /ping checks current liveness. Velocity devices use
feedback.pose_frame for measured placement (null means an unlocalized root).
World contributes optional spatial information; models have no physical pose.

Status: done means complete facts with no outstanding request; ready means its
prerequisites permit work; needs-calibration means an explicit request remains;
blocked means prerequisites are incomplete. Populated sections may be independently
done before their prerequisites. Task-ready checks every applicable required item,
including access, of loaded gating devices. Unloaded devices are optional.

Planning never writes profiles or clears requests. Use calib_write with
needs_calibration:null after successful calibration; incomplete clears are rejected.
The compact view shows actionable details and collapses done/blocked lists;
full:true includes every item's details and dependencies.
