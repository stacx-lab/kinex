Record a task's device observations, actions and session trace.

verbose defaults to true (all fields). Set false to omit empty optional annotations
and bookkeeping; counts, errors and observations remain. JSON uses two-space
indentation for its first two levels and inline deeper values.

- start {devices: {"arm": 10, "wrist_cam": 2}, instruction?}
  Start continuous background capture until stop or the recording size limit. Device
  names and sampling rates are fixed at start; stop before changing them. Native driver
  capture is preferred, otherwise the harness reads at the requested rate. Cameras need
  ffmpeg in the capture environment. Slow sampling holds the last observation in video,
  preserving real elapsed time. The instruction names the task being attempted.
- snap {devices: ["arm", "wrist_cam"], instruction?}
  Read each device once and mark a decision point. During recording this adds a moment;
  otherwise it saves a standalone snapshot. Returns paths and data, never image attachments.
- stop {outcome?, summary?}
  Finish capture and save its products. Outcome is success, failure or partial. The result
  includes the recording directory, per-device video/state paths, and capture errors.
- status {}
  Flush the current recording and list running/recent recordings, their directories,
  capture status, effective rates, frame counts, products and errors.

Recording continues while the agent thinks and between turns. No automatic pause or
segmentation. Ordinary device traffic is also recorded: designated devices under devices/,
others under passive/. snap preserves the original image; background cameras write a single
stream/video.mp4 plus frames.jsonl (RGBD adds lossless depth.mkv); state devices write
stream/state.jsonl. Device unload finishes its capture. A failed device is reported while
other devices continue; max_mb ends and saves the whole recording.

Recordings live under .kinex/recordings/<id>/. No devices are automatically loaded by this tool.

Capture Hz and playback FPS are independent. Check samples, effectiveHz, skipped
(missed capture slots), readErrors and stream.transport_dropped for shortfalls.
Repeated playback frames are not fresh samples. Available capture continues when
the source cannot sustain the rate; warnings identify the affected device.
