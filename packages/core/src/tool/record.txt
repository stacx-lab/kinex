Record typed device topics, explicit snapshots, and session events.

- start {devices: {"arm": 10, "camera": 2}, instruction?}
  Observe all declared topics until stop or the size limit. A value can also be
  {rate: 10, operations: ["joint_state", "joint_target"]} to select operations.
  Output rates are maximum Hz in world time; input command topics, clock and TF
  are retained without rate filtering. Recording never advances world time or
  invokes a device service. Devices and the world clock must already be loaded.
- snap {devices: ["arm", "camera"], instruction?}
  Capture each device's configured observation once, including request/response
  observations. During recording this marks a moment; otherwise it saves a
  standalone snapshot. Returns paths and data without image attachments.
- stop {outcome?, summary?}
  Finalize topic bags and save manifests. Outcome labels are annotations.
- status {}
  Flush metadata and report running/recent recordings, paths, counts and errors.

Each device writes stream/bag/ (rosbag2 SQLite/CDR), operations.json and
samples.jsonl. The bag retains complete typed payloads, including color/depth
images. The index records world time, reset epoch, acquisition time when present,
and receipt time. Clock and TF accompany the samples. Playback uses receipt
order across resets; acquisition stamps remain unchanged. No frames are invented
while the world is paused. rosbag2_py is required in the configured ROS environment.

Recording continues between turns. Host-observed requests and responses are
attributed under devices/ or passive/; direct ROS traffic is in the bag. Device
unload finishes its capture without terminating borrowed nodes. Failures are
per-device; the quota stops the entire recording. Products live under
.kinex/recordings/<id>/. Model recording remains opt-in.

stream.filtered counts intentional output rate filtering. transport_dropped
counts bounded subscription queue losses; samples and capture_errors report
capture progress. verbose=false omits empty annotations, not counts or errors.
